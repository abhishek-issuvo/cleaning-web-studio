A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

What does “starting at $49” mean?

New websites and redesigns start at $49 USD. Your final quote depends on the content and requirements of your project; we confirm the price and scope in writing before work starts. Domain, hosting, paid services, additional work, and any applicable taxes are separate. You’ll receive written terms before committing.

How long does a website take?

We agree on a delivery schedule after reviewing your requirements and content. Timing depends on project availability, receiving your materials, and your feedback.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, logo if you have one, services, service area, contact details, and approved text and images. Only provide content you own or have permission to use.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. The redesign offer rebuilds your content as a one-page HTML/CSS/JavaScript site. It does not modify an existing CMS or migrate accounts, bookings, or other data. Share your current URL so we can confirm fit.

Will I own the website? Can I edit it?

You receive the finished source files at handoff under the agreed project terms. Text and styles can be edited in the files; a visual editor, CMS, and ongoing updates are not included.

Does this include SEO or online booking?

Basic title and description metadata are included. Search rankings and leads are not guaranteed. Booking software, payments, and SEO campaigns are outside this scope and would need a separate agreement.
