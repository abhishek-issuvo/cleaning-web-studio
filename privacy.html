Privacy notice

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This notice explains how ClearSite Studio handles information submitted through this website or by email about our web-design services.

Information you provide

Our inquiry form asks for your name, business name, email address, service choice, and message. You may also provide your website URL. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and anything you choose to attach. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, understand your project, prepare a quote, and manage related business correspondence. Submitting an inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing emails. We do not sell your inquiry information or use it for targeted advertising.

How your inquiry reaches us

The contact form sends the information you enter to Web3Forms, a third-party form-processing service, which processes it and forwards it to our email inbox. Web3Forms may also retain submissions under its own retention policy and use service providers for delivery and spam prevention. Our email provider also processes and stores that correspondence. Web3Forms may process technical information, such as an IP address, as part of delivering and protecting its service. See Web3Forms’ privacy policy for its practices.

Our website hosting provider may process ordinary request logs, including IP addresses, requested pages, and browser information, for delivery, security, and operations. Providers may process information in countries other than your own. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not include analytics, advertising pixels, tracking scripts, or browser storage for inquiries. It does not set cookies through its own code. Hosting and third-party services may process technical data according to their own policies. Links to other websites take you to services governed by their own notices.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry correspondence for as long as needed to respond, manage the resulting business relationship, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. We periodically review information that is no longer needed. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your questions and choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete inquiry information by emailing hello@example.com. We may need to verify your request and may retain records where required or permitted by applicable law. Any rights available to you depend on your location and applicable law.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
